Science Bros

War on the Anti-Science League · 11 missions · 3 bonus modes

The Anti-Science League wants to take over the world. Its leader, King Citrus, the Big Bad Orange, and his general Dr. Placebo hold our scientists in cages. On Placebo Island, blast through the bunker, the acid bridge and the cliff fort (cut the base of an outlined brick or crate structure and it comes down) and destroy the Homeopathitron. In the Detox Desert, storm the Wellness Spa, shoot down the Detoxkopter and kill the Crystal Crawler. On the Cryo Summit, cross the thin ice and stop Dr. Placebo's Snake Oil Tank. In the Dilution Plant, shut down the Succussion Engine. At the Flat Earth Station, bring down the zeppelin. Then fight through the League's five strongholds (the Chemtrail Airfield, the Quartz Caverns, the Miasma Marsh, the Zodiac Observatory and the Perpetual Motion Works) to the Citrus Citadel, and juice King Citrus. Each freed scientist adds one bro to your reserve. From the title menu you can also hold the Lab Siege, run the Boss Rush or land on the Moon Base.
