Science Bros

Operation Infinite Dilution · 4 missions

Dr. Placebo wants to dilute the world's water until no medicine works. His quack army holds eleven scientists in cages. On Placebo Island, blast through the bunker, the acid bridge and the cliff fort (cut a structure loose and it comes down) and destroy the Homeopathitron. In the Detox Desert, storm the Wellness Spa, shoot down the Detoxkopter and kill the Crystal Crawler. On the Cryo Summit, cross the thin ice and stop Dr. Placebo's Snake Oil Tank. In the Dilution Plant, shut down the Succussion Engine. Each freed scientist adds one bro to your reserve.
