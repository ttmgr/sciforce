Science Bros

Level 1 · Placebo Island

Dr. Placebo's quack army holds four scientists in cages across Placebo Island. Free them, blast through the bunker, the acid bridge and the cliff fort, destroy the Homeopathitron and reach the extraction chopper. Each freed scientist adds one bro to your reserve.
