Glossary

A small glossary of project-specific terms that keep showing up in commits, memos, and code (things like 'dual_threshold' or 'competitive_mapping'). Each entry pairs the term with a short definition so you don't have to reverse-engineer the meaning from context months down the line.

:bro
a playable scientist (46 in all, 3 of them melee bros). A new run starts with START_BROS (10) random bros; the rest are unlocked by freeing them from cages. After a death the next bro is random, never the one who…
:quack
any enemy. Bosses are separate and have their own wake-up rule.
:T
tile size in pixels (16). The tile arrays are LW x LH (560 x 40). playW is the playable width of the current level: 560 for the 20 campaign missions, 376 for random missions and bonus modes; columns from…
:GH
ground height row (22). Rows 0-21 are the classic surface level; rows 22-38 are earth and row 39 is steel bedrock, filled by deepen().
:stretch
the extra columns inserted before each boss arena: stretchW is 324 (STRETCH) in campaign missions and 140 (OLD_STRETCH) elsewhere. Levels are built 236 wide, then slideArena() moves the arena right from…
:fork
a stretch piece (stretchFork) with a steel high road reached by ladders and a low road under it. Campaign only, never in random maps.
:underground zone
a mine, bunker, sewer, cavern or lab (UG_KINDS) below row 22, reached by shaft, ladder, lift or cracked floor.
:random mission
a map made from scratch by genLevel(seed, diff) from segment and arena tables (SEG_META, ARENA_META). Same seed gives the same tiles; ?seed= replays one.
:aim
holding the aim key keeps the facing fixed so the bro can walk backwards and still shoot forwards; Up tilts the gun. aimShots applies it; weapons in NO_AIM keep their own path.
:blame
global that records who caused a hit, so the player's own weapons never kill the player.
:DESTRUCT / wreck()
per-weapon terrain damage by material (earth, brick, crate, ice, steel). Steel above the bedrock row has 10 hp and only weapons with a DESTRUCT s value dent it; bedrock and boss arenas never break.
:pickup gun / one-use special
GUNS (8 guns) drop from gun crates; ONCE specials (confetti, beach, spud) replace the bro's next special press once (P.once).
:BOSS_MUL
per-weapon damage multiplier against bosses, tuned so every bro can beat every boss.
:seedN
a fixed seed per mission or mode, so inserting missions (round 5 put two before the Citadel) does not change the others; never seed by level index.
:difficulty / density
two separate saved settings. DIFFS (EASY, NORMAL, HARD, INSANE) changes how hard fights are; DENS / densMul (25% to 300%) changes how many quacks spawn. Campaign missions spawn 1.5x the base (CAMPAIGN_DENS),…
:gore
setting 0 (off), 1 (on) or 2 (extra). Robots and bosses leak their own colour (LEAK) instead of blood.
:test hooks
window.__SB_TEST__ and window.__SB__, filled by Object.assign lines at the bottom of the script. Values that change must be exposed as functions, not copied values.
